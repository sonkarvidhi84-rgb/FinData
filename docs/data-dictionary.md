## Views

### `v_price_adjusted`

NSE prices with split/dividend-adjusted OHLC: `adjusted_x = x * cumulative_factor`, where the factor comes from the `adj_factor_segment` covering the trade date (1.0 when none).

- **Grain:** one row per ISIN
